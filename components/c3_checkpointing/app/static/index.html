How the difficulty and depth are decided

C3 predicts how hard it will be to resume (Low / Medium / High) when you are interrupted. The prediction decides how much the checkpoint asks for and how long the restart summary is.

FactorPoints
Time away: under 15 min / 15-59 min / 1-4 h / 4 h or more0 / 1 / 2 / 3
Task complexity: simple / moderate / complex0 / 1 / 2
Unfinished items: 0-1 / 2-4 / 5 or more0 / 1 / 2
Interrupted by another work task+1

Score 0-2 = Low (short checkpoint), 3-4 = Medium (standard), 5-8 = High (full checkpoint and fuller summary).

This is a preliminary, hand-set rule. Our Model 1b trained on the Hirsch et al. lab data found the same direction (longer interruptions and more complex tasks raise resumption difficulty), but its signal is modest and the lab interruptions last seconds, not hours. The thresholds will be recalibrated with data from our participant study.
