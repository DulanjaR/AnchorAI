    How this estimate was made

    Each signal is compared with Dilani's own mean and spread over the last 14 days. Only deviations in the unhelpful direction count, capped at 3 spreads. The weighted total gives a strain share, and capacity is 100 minus that share. Bands: High 72 and above, Moderate 52 to 71, Low 32 to 51, At Risk below 32. At Risk is also shown when capacity is at least 22 points below what the chosen work needs (Light 35, Standard 55, Demanding 70). All cut-offs are provisional.

    Evidence and limits

    What this prototype does, what it does not, and what the data so far shows.

    What C2 does

    Estimates current readiness for mentally demanding work from objective signals, compared with the person's own baseline, and suggests a protective action. The person accepts, edits or rejects it.

    What C2 does not do

    It does not diagnose, measure biological capacity, set deadlines or rearrange schedules (that belongs to C1). Energy, focus, stress and fatigue are latent states estimated from signals, never read directly from a phone.

    Evidence so far (public data, preliminary)

    StudentLife (47 students): phone-only features gave a weak, unstable stress signal (AUC 0.55 to 0.63 with people held out).

    SWELL-KW (25 people): the strong score (AUC 0.86) came from study design, because the neutral condition is always the first block. Block order alone scored 1.00, and the order-balanced comparison was near chance.

    Conclusion. Public data cannot validate capacity labels. High, Moderate, Low and At Risk will be calibrated and validated on working-student data (self-report, behaviour, task outcome, repeated weeks).
